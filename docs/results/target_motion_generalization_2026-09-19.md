## 1. Question

Does a policy frozen on the historical target-motion lineage remain valid when the target motion law changes? Three generalization arms (E0 static, E1 constant-velocity, E2 obstacle-aware) are compared against **arm H**, the historical lineage the checkpoint was actually trained on. None of E0/E1/E2 is the training distribution, which is why H exists.

## 8. Visibility / reacquisition mechanism

Pooled step-weighted target visibility (pooled step-weighted: sum(visible_steps)/sum(observation_steps) over all 12 cells of the arm):

> **Estimator caveat.** Pooled step-weighting weights LONGER episodes more heavily. Timeout episodes run the full 600-step budget while captures end early, so a timeout-heavy arm is pulled toward its timeout-episode visibility. This is NOT an unconditioned per-episode mean and must not be presented as one. The per-outcome-class breakdown is given alongside precisely so the aggregate is readable.

| arm | overall | capture episodes | crash | timeout |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| E0 static | 0.2202 | 0.3378 | 0.0206 | 0.0016 |
| E1 constant-velocity | 0.2502 | 0.3310 | 0.0793 | 0.0059 |
| H historical (in-distribution reference) | 0.2889 | 0.3518 | 0.0854 | 0.0079 |
| E2 obstacle-aware | 0.3215 | 0.3598 | 0.1324 | 0.0254 |

The ordering matches the capture ordering, and E0's timeout episodes carry a visibility of **0.0016** — the target is essentially never observed in the episodes that time out.

> The results are consistent with a visibility/reacquisition-mediated mechanism: a stationary target that leaves the observable region does not move back into view, whereas moving target laws create additional re-observation opportunities.

This is an association. **No mediation or counterfactual experiment was run**, so visibility is not claimed to causally explain the full performance difference.

## 10. Retraining verdict

```text
NO_RETRAINING_JUSTIFIED_FOR_E2_TARGET_MOTION_SHIFT
```

E2, the obstacle-aware target arm, did not degrade the frozen policy's close-approach rate relative to the in-distribution reference H. The seed-paired effect was +1.33 pp (BCa95 [+1.15, +1.50], 3/3 seeds positive).

**Scope.** This addresses TARGET-MOTION SHIFT ONLY, for this checkpoint, this arena and these four densities. It does not say the policy can never benefit from retraining, and it says nothing about any other environment change.

**E0/E1.** E0 (-3.58 pp) and E1 (-2.82 pp) DID show lower capture than H with intervals excluding zero. Those are real reductions. No preregistered threshold exists to classify them as material or immaterial, so they are reported as measured effects rather than as a pass or fail.

**Threshold provenance.** The 5 pp rule is `POST_HOC DECISION CONTEXT`, not preregistered. The preliminary report wrongly called the 5 pp rule preregistered and issued the verdict NO_RETRAIN_NEEDED. Both are withdrawn here.

## 11. Limitations

* Three evaluation seeds. Exact permutation inference cannot reach conventional significance.
* One checkpoint, one arena, four densities. No claim generalizes beyond them.
* The preregistered distance primary is unavailable; only capture_rate carries the primary.
* Density contrasts are exploratory and were not tested under a preregistered interaction model.
* The visibility mechanism is an association, not a demonstrated cause.
* Simulation only. No real-flight validation of anything here.
* E2 is not shown to be universally easier; the finding is specific to this frozen policy.
