# ETH ds5 E3, split into E3-S and E3-P

E3 was one stage: "separate detector-box error from pose/viewpoint-associated size variation". The
2026-09-10 investigation showed that half of that is supported by the evidence and half is not, so the
stage is split. E3-S is COMPLETE. The latest reliability verdict is ATTITUDE_NOT_RELIABLE;
E3-P decomposition remains BLOCKED. Historical prerequisites below are superseded by the gate correction.

## What each stage asks

**E3-S — how well does apparent size recover metric range?**
Given a measurement of the target's apparent size in cam0 and a focal length, how accurately is the
target's slant range recovered, and how does that error behave across range? It is a performance
question about a size-based range estimator. It is not a question about why the error occurs.

**E3-P — how much of the apparent-size variation is viewpoint, not range?**
Of the residual size variation that E3-S leaves unexplained, how much is attributable to the target's
attitude relative to the camera's viewing direction? This needs the target's attitude and the camera's
orientation in the same frame at the same instant.

## Historical prerequisites — superseded by the corrected gate below

| requirement | E3-S | E3-P |
|---|---|---|
| target position ground truth | needed, available | needed, available |
| focal length | needed, available and verified against its own chessboard images | needed |
| camera orientation | **not needed** | needed, **not available**: reprojection leaves 5.6 px against 0.198 px trajectory-smoothness residual (not accuracy) |
| target attitude ground truth | not needed | needed, published but unverified here |
| frame-to-pose time alignment | weakly needed, see below | strongly needed, **unresolved**: an unexplained −3.6 frame (−120 ms) offset |
| lens distortion model | weakly needed, treated as a sensitivity | needed for viewing-angle geometry |

E3-S depends on range, which is a scalar along the line of sight. A 120 ms timing error moves the range
by the radial speed times 0.12 s, well under one percent at these ranges, and this is measured as a
pre-registered sensitivity rather than assumed. E3-P depends on angles between two orientations, which
the unvalidated camera model cannot supply.

## What is established and what is not

Established: the target is drone0 (apparent size follows drone0's ground-truth range to a 6.2% held-out
median relative error over 3,107 frames and 9 blocks from 30.7 to 108.4 m, which needs no camera
orientation; the "six-armed airframe" cue stated here on 2026-09-10 is WITHDRAWN, all three aircraft are
quadrotors, and the 0.6 degree angular residual is corroboration at most because the fit producing it
fails the calibration gate; see results/eth_ds5_intake_2026-09-10/CORRECTION_2026-09-11_rotor_count.md);
position ground truth with published centimetre-level uncertainty; a focal length that reproduces its own
calibration images to 1.245 px held-out; a 0.196 px trajectory-smoothness residual measured without ground truth, not an accuracy bound.

Not established: the frame-to-pose time alignment beyond a fitted offset; the camera orientation; the
lens distortion as it applies to ds5 cam0; the attitude convention of the published roll, pitch and yaw;
whether the residual inconsistency lives in the timestamps, the fused pose or the camera.

## E3-S result

`SIZE_RANGE_USABLE`, all four gates passed. Held-out median absolute relative range error **6.2 %** at
the block level, stability 9.3 points, across 3107 frames in 9 blocks from 31 to 108 m. The unresolved
−120 ms offset moves it by 0.3 points. Range and time are confounded in this flight, so the per-bin
numbers are not a range effect. Preregistration:
[PREREGISTRATION.md](../../results/eth_ds5_e3s_2026-09-10/PREREGISTRATION.md), fixed and committed
(572314c) before the analysis ran. Results and limits:
[README](../../results/eth_ds5_e3s_2026-09-10/README.md).

## E3-P gate, corrected 2026-09-10 after measuring it

The four conditions first written here overstated two of them. The correction rests on a geometry and
dynamics calculation, not on any E3-S result. Receipt and detail:
[results/eth_ds5_e3p_gate_2026-09-10/](../../results/eth_ds5_e3p_gate_2026-09-10/README.md).

**Withdrawn as preconditions.** The camera's orientation is not needed: the aspect angle between the line
of sight and the target's body axes follows from two known positions and the published attitude, and the
camera's own rotation never enters an area-based size measure. Timing is not binding either: the −120 ms
offset moves the aspect angle by 0.88° at p90 against a 30.8° span, 2.9 %.

**The conditions that decide the gate, with their measured status:**

| condition | status |
|---|---|
| C1 attitude convention identified from the data | **clear.** Intrinsic `xyz`, body to NED, thrust along body −z, mapped to ENU. Median direction error against ground-truth acceleration 17.7°, beating the runner-up reading by 16.3° |
| C2/C2b attitude reliability | **fails.** The magnitude-only form gives correlation 0.18; the drag-corrected vector form gives thrust gain 0.73, drag −0.017, correlation 0.64, but the drag term improves the residual by 4.4 % against a required 20 % |
| C3 aspect and range separable | **clear.** Correlation 0.21; six range-by-aspect cells with at least 20 frames from at least two blocks; every range bin spans more than 20° of aspect |
| C4 timing not binding | **clear.** 2.9 % of the aspect span, against a 5 % threshold |

**Verdict: `E3P_GATE_BLOCKED` on the reliability condition alone.** A third form was then designed to ask
the right question — does the attitude carry real frame-specific motion information — and
[preregistered before it ran](../../results/eth_ds5_e3p_reliability_2026-09-10/PREREGISTRATION.md). It
requires the attitude to beat 200 permutations of the attitude-to-frame assignment, out of sample, at
every one of six differentiation windows, so the window cannot be chosen after the fact.

**Authenticity passed everywhere**: the worst of twelve cells scores 0.163 against a null 99th percentile
of 0.043, and the thrust gain is stable at 0.70 to 0.75 throughout. **The criterion still fails**, on the
0.35 correlation floor at the two shortest windows, where correlation rises monotonically with window
length from 0.24 to 0.89 — the signature of the differentiator's noise rather than the attitude's. The
all-or-nothing sweep was committed in advance precisely so that this pattern could not be read into a
pass. [Result](../../results/eth_ds5_e3p_reliability_2026-09-10/README.md).

No fourth variant will be written. Having seen these numbers, no honest preregistration of a different
floor for this test on this dataset remains possible; what is still available is independent evidence that
does not reuse the acceleration test, such as whether the airframe's projected orientation at close range
tracks the published attitude.

## Current stop condition

The third reliability test has already run and failed. It is not a pending next step.
Do not change its floor, select a passing window or run a fourth variant to reopen it.
Independent evidence would require separate review; no such execution is authorised here.

When the gate opens, E3-P is preregistered separately, before any result is inspected, to separate size
change explained by range from size change associated with viewing direction. **E3-S results must not be
used to choose E3-P's hypotheses or thresholds.** The available aspect range is also narrower than the
flight's full span: the populated cells sit between 60° and 90°, which E3-P's design has to account for.
